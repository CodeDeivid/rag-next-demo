# Remote Work Policy

_Fictional handbook used to demo the app. Any resemblance to a real company is accidental._

## Who can work remotely

Every employee who has finished the 90-day onboarding period can work remotely up to four days a week.
Team leads may ask for one fixed office day so the team overlaps in person.

## Core hours

Core hours are 10:00 to 16:00 in the employee's local time zone. Outside core hours, people organise their
day as they prefer, as long as meetings are announced at least 24 hours in advance.

## Equipment

The company provides a laptop, a monitor and a headset. Employees who work remotely at least three days a
week receive a one-time home office allowance of 400 USD for a chair or desk, paid through the expense
process.

## Working from another country

Working from another country is allowed for up to 30 days per calendar year. Longer stays need written
approval from People Ops because of tax and labour rules.
