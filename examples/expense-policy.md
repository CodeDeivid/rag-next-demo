# Expense Policy

_Fictional handbook used to demo the app. Any resemblance to a real company is accidental._

## What can be reimbursed

Work travel, client meals, conference tickets, books and online courses related to the employee's role, and
the home office allowance described in the remote work policy.

## Limits

- Meals with clients: up to 60 USD per person, alcohol not included.
- Hotels: up to 180 USD per night, or the conference hotel when it is more expensive.
- Learning budget: 1,000 USD per employee per calendar year. Unused budget does not roll over.

## How to submit

Submit expenses within 30 days with a photo of the receipt. Expenses above 500 USD need the manager's
approval before the purchase. Reimbursements are paid with the next monthly salary.

## Not reimbursed

Fines, personal entertainment, upgrades to business class, and anything bought without a receipt.
